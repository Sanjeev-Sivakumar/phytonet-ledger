## 1. Executive Summary & Biophysical Sensing Architecture

PhytoNet Ledger (PNL) interfaces directly with the living vascular cambium of mature arboreal specimens to verify biophysical vitality, derive continuous chaotic entropy (Biological Nonce), and quantify photosynthetic transpiration fluxes for on-chain carbon sequestration certification.

The hardware subsystem executes a **Dual-Channel Active Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy (EIS) and Micro-potential Galvanic Loop**:
1. **Dynamic Excitation Channel:** Injects a micro-amperage, high-frequency AC sinusoidal challenge signal into the cambium tissue to assess cellular wall membrane capacitance and apoplast resistance.
2. **Dynamic Response & Sap Flux Channel:** Concurrently reads the differential phase response and the endogenous action/resting potentials generated by electro-osmotic ionic transport (potassium $K^+$, chloride $Cl^-$, and sucrose transport in phloem sieve tube elements).
3. **Soil Matrix Ground Reference:** Eliminates 50/60 Hz common-mode mains interference and earth galvanic loops via a direct rhizosphere copper anchor.

## 3. Signal Conditioning & Electrochemical Protection Circuitry

To prevent tissue necrosis, galvanic polarization, and galvanic electro-etching of the electrodes during continuous multi-season deployment, the following front-end conditioning topology is implemented:

```
GPIO 25 (DAC) ---[ 100nF C_block ]---[ 100kΩ R_limit ]----+----> Probe B (Excitation)
                                                            |
                                                       [ 1MΩ R_shunt ]
                                                            |
                                                           GND

Probe A (Sensing) ---[ 10MΩ Input Buffer ]----+----> [ GPIO 32 / ADC1_CH4 ]
                                            |
                                       [ 47pF C_filter ]
                                            |
                                           GND (Rhizosphere Stake)
```

1. **Galvanic Polarization Elimination:** The **100nF non-polarized ceramic capacitor (C_block)** ensures zero net DC current injection into the cambium, completely eliminating electrolytic tissue burns or ion accumulation.
2. **Current Limiting:** The **100kΩ series resistor (R_limit)** clamps total challenge current below $5\,\mu\text{A}$, well below the threshold of plant stress response.
3. **Anti-Aliasing & High-Frequency Shunt:** A **47pF capacitor** on the ADC input shunts environmental RF / WiFi burst noise to the rhizosphere ground.

## 4. Electrode Geometry & Tree Inoculation Protocol

- **Probe Metallurgy:** Medical-grade Titanium or Silver/Silver-Chloride ($\text{Ag/AgCl}$) needle electrodes (0.45 mm outer diameter).
- **Insertion Depth:** 
  - Outer bark thickness measured with increment borer (typically 2.0 mm to 6.0 mm).
  - Micro-probes inserted perpendicular to the trunk grain into the vascular cambium zone between xylem and secondary phloem.
  - Probe spacing: **45 mm axial separation** along the stem axis to trace vertical sap velocity vectors.
- **Sterilization & Sealant:** Electrodes sanitized with 70% isopropyl alcohol prior to insertion; wound sealed with cold-applied horticultural beeswax to prevent fungal pathogen entry and micro-evaporation drying.

## 5. TinyML Edge Noise-Rejection & Proof-of-Vascular-Integrity (PoVI) Logic

The on-board ESP32 firmware executes a 3-stage TinyML DSP pipeline before packing serial JSON frames:
1. **60-Hz / 50-Hz Notch Filter:** Digital biquad IIR filter suppressing 50/60 Hz grid interference induced in tree trunks.
2. **Phase Angle & Impedance Extraction:** Fast Zero-Crossing & Peak-to-Peak detector computes:
   $$Z_{\text{cellular}} = \frac{V_{\text{response}}}{I_{\text{excitation}}}$$
   $$\Delta\theta = 2\pi f \cdot \Delta t$$
3. **Biological Nonce Chaotic Accumulator:** The 4 least significant bits (LSBs) of raw ADC conversions, subject to thermal cambium quantum fluctuations and ion channel gating noise, are XOR-folded into a 256-bit entropy pool to produce an unforgeable, unrepeatable biological seed.
4. **Wire-Cut / Necrosis Watchdog:** If cellular impedance drops below $1.0\,\text{k}\Omega$ (short/water intrusion) or phase angle flattens to $0.0^\circ$ with variance $< 0.05\,\text{mV}$ (disconnected electrode or dead desiccated tissue), the firmware flags `STATUS_POVI_BURN_TRIGGERED` and asserts a local tamper alarm.
